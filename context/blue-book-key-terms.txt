Blue Book 2025 v3.6 — Key Terms for CadetFlow Development
Source: context/Blue Book - 2025 v.3.6.pdf
Last reviewed: 2026-06-15

Use this file when building features that touch discipline, conduct, reports, or
cadet leadership. Internal DB codes (offense_code 1/2a/3b, policy_category 1-3)
may differ from user-facing labels below.


================================================================================
DISCIPLINE TERMINOLOGY
================================================================================

Demerit Report (Stick)
  - Official name: "Demerit Report"; cadets also call them "Sticks"
  - Submitted to the Commandant's Office with brief offense + detailed narrative
  - Consolidated daily on the "green sheet" (bulletin board + email, 24 hours)

Green Sheet
  - Daily published list of outstanding demerit reports
  - UI label: "Green Sheet" (not "Daily Reports" in user-facing copy)

Demerit Credits
  - 15 credits per cadet at the start of each academic term
  - Demerits within credits = no punishment tours required
  - Each demerit over credits = one Penalty Tour
  - Credits do NOT apply to Category III offenses

Penalty Tour
  - User-facing term for owed punishment tours (prefer over generic "Tour Balance")
  - Display: "Penalty Tours Owed" or "Tours Owed"


================================================================================
OFFENSE CATEGORIES (Roman numerals in UI)
================================================================================

Display in UI: Category I, Category II, Category III
Internal policy_category: 1, 2, 3
Internal offense_code tiers (app-only granularity):

  Code  policy_cat  Demerits   Blue Book basis
  ----  ----------  --------   ----------------
  0     0           0          App-only warnings (not in Blue Book)
  1     1           3          Category I — fixed 3 demerits
  2a    2           6          Category II — serious (6-dem tier)
  2b    2           10         Category II — serious (10-dem tier)
  3a    3           15         Category III — major (15+ demerits)
  3b    3           25         Category III — operational tier
  3c    3           35         Category III — operational tier

Category I
  - Minor offenses; 3 demerits each
  - Tour credits may apply

Category II
  - Serious offenses; 6 or 10 demerits
  - C.Q. violations may also require loss of leave
  - Tour credits may apply

Category III
  - Major/very serious; 15+ demerits (Commandant may set case-by-case)
  - Tour credits do NOT apply — immediate penalty tours
  - Cadet must fill out a special report for TAC on day posted
  - TAC recommends punishment to Commandant

Offense subject groups (offense_group field — use Blue Book headings):
  Appearance, Arms, Behavior in Class, Campus, Conduct, Room/Barracks,
  When in Ranks, Attitude, Chapel, Study C.Q., Computers, Duty,
  Equipment/Property, Hygiene, Infirmary, Leaves/Day Passes, Dining Hall,
  Off Limits, Taps, Tobacco, Tribunal, Honor Code Violations


================================================================================
CONDUCT LEVELS
================================================================================

Term conduct resets to Exemplary each new academic term; year demerits do NOT reset.
Displayed conduct = least commendable level from term OR year thresholds.

  Level            Term demerits   Year demerits
  ---------------  --------------  -------------
  Exemplary        0–6             0–30
  Commendable      7–18            31–90
  Satisfactory     19–30           91–150
  Deficient        31–42           151–210
  Unsatisfactory   43+             211+

UI label: "Conduct Level" (section title in Blue Book: "Conduct Levels")

Counseling / action thresholds (term):
  7+   Informal counseling (TAC); parent email
  13+  Additional informal counseling; Conduct Expectations Agreement warning
  19+  Conduct Expectations Agreement + parent conference
  25+  Formal counseling; approaching Deficient
  31+  Probation Letter (Deputy Commandant); leave suspended
  37+  Behavioral Contract; CRB warning
  43+  Unsatisfactory; Commandant's Review Board (CRB)
  55+  Referral to Academy President (term) / 275+ (year)
  67+  Tribunal threshold (term) / 335+ (year) — basis for dismissal

Conduct Probation (Blue Book)
  - Deficient or Unsatisfactory conduct with assigned probation letter


================================================================================
REPORT WORKFLOW & APPEALS
================================================================================

Submission routing (cadet-prepared reports):
  Cadet officer/NCO → cadet chain of command → Company Commander → screening

Category I & II appeals:
  - Company Commander hears explanations at morning break
  - Company TAC is appellate authority

Category III:
  - Company TAC recommends punishment to Commandant
  - Commandant makes final decision

Appeals (general):
  - Route through chain of command
  - Cadet may submit complaint directly to Company TAC if improperly treated


================================================================================
CHAIN OF COMMAND (Cadet organization)
================================================================================

Corps structure: Infantry Battalion
  - Battalion Staff: Battalion CO, XO, CSM, S-1 through S-7, Adjutant
  - Four Rifle Companies: A, B, C, D (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta)

Company-level cadet roles (use these names in UI):
  - Company Commander
  - Company Executive Officer (XO)
  - First Sergeant (1SG)
  - Platoon Leader
  - Platoon Sergeant
  - Squad Leader

Adult staff (not cadet chain):
  - Commandant / Deputy Commandant / Commandant of Cadets
  - Company TAC (not just "TAC Officer" when company-scoped)
  - Commandant's Office (final authority for demerit reports)

CadetFlow approval groups map to chain levels internally; prefer role titles
in user-facing copy over abstract names like "Squad Staff" or "Co. Staff".

Roles NOT in Blue Book leadership chain:
  - Laundry Sergeant (remove or treat as app-specific if used)


================================================================================
PUNISHMENTS (reference)
================================================================================

Types: Verbal correction, Written warning, Incentive Training, Demerits,
Extra duty tours, Restriction (loss of leave/day pass), Reduction in rank,
Probation, Commandant's Review Board (CRB), Tribunal
